Getting There

By Air & By Road

By Air

Nearest Airports

The nearest major airport is Philadelphia International (PHL), approximately 45 minutes from the venue. Newark Liberty (EWR) is also an option, about 90 minutes away.

By Road

Parking & Directions

Aldie Mansion is located at 85 Old Dublin Pike, Doylestown, PA 18901. On-site parking is available for the wedding ceremony and reception. Guests attending the Welcome / Rehearsal Dinner at the Doylestown Inn will use street parking.

Make a Weekend of It

Explore Doylestown

A charming town in Bucks County, full of history, culture, and great food.

Mercer Museum

A super unique castle-like museum filled with early American tools and artifacts… it's said that visiting here kind of feels like stepping into a time capsule!

Fonthill Castle

Right next door, an eclectic concrete castle built by Henry Mercer. Very artsy, very unusual.

Bowman's Hill Wildflower Preserve

Gorgeous native plants, trails, and a scenic tower view (a short drive away).

Downtown Doylestown

Cute, walkable area with boutiques, bookstores, coffee shops, and little galleries.

Siren Records

A fun indie record shop in the area, if you're into music.

County Theater

Historic indie movie theater… super charming.
