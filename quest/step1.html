A Quest Begins

A voice booms from beyond the veil. Three trials stand between you and a hidden treasure. Steel yourself…

“A Dark Knight in miniature form,
Through every campaign he hath been born.
No matter the realm, no matter the quest,
This painted champion stands above the rest.

What figure doth your hero set upon the field of battle?
Name the knight, then seek his likeness in the Gallery of Memories.”

Hint  Journey to the Gallery and find the hero in disguise. Click upon his image, and speak his name to prove your worth.
