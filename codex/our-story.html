I.Our Story

The story of how two souls found each other.

Part One · How It Started

Once, as roommates…

Josh and Ashley's story began in the most unexpected way… as roommates. What started as a simple living arrangement quickly grew into a close friendship, built on laughter, late-night conversations, and a shared love of all things nerdy and magical. From teaming up in video games to embarking on epic Dungeons & Dragons campaigns, exploring fantasy worlds, and wandering the Renaissance Faire together, their bond only deepened over time.

Part Two · Falling

Quests, and quests of the heart.

Somewhere between quests and quests of the heart, friendship turned into something more… and before they knew it, they had fallen deeply in love. Now, they're excited to begin their greatest adventure yet… together, around a Dungeons & Dragons table and far beyond.

III. The Proposal

November 25 · 2025 · Annandale Falls, Grenada

What was meant to be a simple visit to a waterfall became one of the most meaningful moments of Ashley and Josh's story.

On November 25, 2025, Josh proposed at Annandale Falls in Grenada, along a quiet, private trail leading to the top of the waterfall. Although Ashley had been part of the ring process and knew about the setting Josh had chosen, as well as the diamond generously gifted by his mother, she intentionally chose not to see them together until the proposal… making that moment all the more special.

Even so, she was convinced it wouldn't happen on this trip. Josh had carefully left the ring packaging behind at home, and she believed the ring had been left there too.

Before heading toward the falls, they shared lunch at the waterfall café with her dad, David, her brother, Scott, and her aunt, Marcie. While Ashley enjoyed the moment, completely unaware of what was coming, Josh was so nervous he could barely eat.

When they arrived at the entrance to the waterfall, the first thing they encountered was a local monkey named Ares, who climbed onto Josh's shoulder… an unexpected and unforgettable start to what would become one of the most important moments of their lives.

Soon after, they made their way up the secluded trail. Surrounded by the quiet of the rainforest, Josh dropped to one knee on the muddy ground and opened the ring box before he could even begin to speak. Overcome with emotion, Ashley said "yes, yes, a million times yes!" before he had the chance to ask. Laughing, Josh gently told her he still had to ask, and shared that he had never known true love until her, never thought he would meet someone like her, and that waking up beside her every day made him the luckiest man alive. Then, finally, he asked her to marry him… and she said yes, of course, all over again.

They celebrated with champagne that had been thoughtfully tucked away in the van, soaking in the joy of the moment together before returning to the house and continuing the celebration later that evening at a lively outdoor bingo hall… an unforgettable end to an already unforgettable day.

And so the adventure begins… On November 13, 2026, Joshua and Ashley step into a new chapter filled with shared dreams, quiet moments, loud laughter, and a lifetime of love. What started as two separate stories now becomes one, woven together with intention, joy, whimsy, and unwavering commitment.
