Shared foundation.

Every variation keeps these constants so the site stays recognisable as your site — only the aesthetic language changes.

  • PaletteParchment, burgundy-vine, gold. Same family. Different saturation + role per variation.
  • TypeCormorant Garamond (display) + EB Garamond (body) + JetBrains Mono (captions). All free, all durable, none overused.
  • CopyEvery word preserved verbatim — including the full proposal story and all FAQs.
  • PhotosSame image set from the repo. Framing, crop and treatment differs per variation.
  • D&D levelDialled to 60/100 — felt, not costumed. The d20, castle and shield remain as easter eggs to Secret Lair and the Quest.
  • PagesHome + Our Story + Details are fully redesigned per variation; remaining pages inherit each variation's stylesheet.
